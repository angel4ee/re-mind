OUR GOALS FOR LASTING IMPACT

OBJECTIVES

SO1

To develop concept and training material for mindfulness-based experience model in museums for reaching different generations: children, Gen Z and older adults.

SO2

To build the capacity of museums professionals with transferable mindfulness techniques and digital wellbeing skills and competencies tailored to intergenerational audience engagement and integrated into Smart Learning Ecosystem.

SO3

To educate and empower intergenerational audiences on mindful engagement and digital wellbeing practices in museums.

SO4

To implement and validate the Mindful Museum capacity-building model – to establish a transferable capacity-building model that enables museums to sustainably integrate mindful museum experience and empower intergenerational audience engagement.

5

SO5

To sustainably disseminate the RE-MIND model and tools at the European level targeting museum networks, policymakers, cultural institutions and general public.

SO6

To foster intergenerational solidarity and social participation by embedding collaborative activities into museum experiences.
