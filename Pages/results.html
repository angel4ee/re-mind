DELIVERING TOOLS, KNOWLEDGE, AND LASTING IMPACT

RESULTS

PROJECT RESULTS

Throughout its three-year implementation, RE-MIND will develop and validate an innovative set of tools, methodologies, and resources that enable museums across Europe to become more inclusive, mindful, and digitally balanced cultural spaces.

Mindful Museums Model

The project’s flagship result will be the Mindful Museum Model - an innovative, evidence-based framework that integrates mindfulness practices, calm technology, digital wellbeing, and intergenerational engagement into museum education and audience development. Designed to be transferable and scalable, the model will provide museums with practical guidance for creating more meaningful visitor experiences.

RE-MIND Training Toolkit

A comprehensive Training Toolkit will be developed to support museum professionals in implementing the Mindful Museum approach. The toolkit will include methodologies, practical exercises, guidelines, multimedia learning resources, and best practices for designing mindful museum experiences and engaging diverse audiences.

Smart Learning Ecosystem

RE-MIND will establish an online Smart Learning Ecosystem, offering accessible, self-paced learning opportunities for museum professionals across Europe. The platform will feature interactive training modules, digital resources, case studies, and collaborative learning opportunities while supporting continuous professional development through micro-credentialing.

Micro-Credentials and Professional Recognition

Museum professionals completing the training programme will be able to earn micro-credentials recognising newly acquired competences in areas such as mindfulness-based audience engagement, digital wellbeing, and intergenerational facilitation. These credentials will be aligned with European competence frameworks, enhancing the recognition and portability of the acquired skills.

Capacity Building for Museum Professionals

The project will strengthen the capacities of museum staff by equipping them with innovative methods and practical skills to: Design mindful and inclusive museum experiences; Promote healthy and balanced use of digital technologies; Engage children, young people, older adults, and families through intergenerational activities; Foster emotional wellbeing and social inclusion within museum environments.

Educational Activities and Intergenerational Programmes

RE-MIND will design and pilot a range of educational activities, mindfulness sessions, storytelling experiences, and intergenerational workshops that actively involve children, Generation Z, older adults, and museum professionals in co-creating museum experiences.

Pilot Implementation and Validation

The Mindful Museum Model will be tested and validated through pilot activities and showroom events in partner museums across Europe. These real-life demonstrations will provide valuable feedback, refine the methodology, and showcase its practical application in different cultural contexts.

Mindful Museum Recognition

The project will introduce a “Mindful Museum” recognition scheme, acknowledging museums that successfully adopt the RE-MIND methodology and demonstrate excellence in promoting digital wellbeing, inclusion, and intergenerational engagement.

Mindful Museums Network

To ensure the long-term sustainability of the project, RE-MIND will establish the Mindful Museums Network, bringing together museums, cultural organisations, educators, and stakeholders interested in promoting mindful and inclusive cultural participation. The network will facilitate knowledge exchange, collaboration, and the continued dissemination of project results beyond the project’s lifetime.

Policy Recommendations and European Outreach

RE-MIND will produce recommendations and practical guidance for cultural institutions, museum networks, and policymakers, contributing to the development of more inclusive, digitally responsible, and wellbeing-oriented cultural policies across Europe.

Long-Term Impact

By the end of the project, RE-MIND will provide museums with a validated and sustainable capacity-building model that enhances audience engagement, supports digital wellbeing, promotes intergenerational dialogue, and strengthens the role of museums as spaces for learning, reflection, and community wellbeing across Europe.
